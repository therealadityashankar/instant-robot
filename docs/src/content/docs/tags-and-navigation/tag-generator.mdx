## Tag Categories & Placement Rules

### 1. Navigation Station Cards (Tags 200 & 201)
- **Pair Structure**: Contains Marker 200 on the left and Marker 201 on the right, with a clear human-readable title and AI context description between them.
- **Placement**: Stick onto the vertical front face of docking stations, tables, or storage shelves at approximately **5–10 cm above floor level** so the base camera has a direct line of sight.
- **Why Two Markers?**: The dual-marker pair provides exact scale, distance, and heading angle to square the robot directly in front of the target.

### 2. Moveable Object / Prop Tags (Tags 1 – 199)
- **Placement**: Attach directly to the top or side of objects to pick up (apples, blocks, tools, cups).
- **Orientation**: Align the top arrow pointing towards the front/top of the object. When the arm camera observes the tag, the analytical IK solver automatically aligns the gripper jaws to grasp along the approach axis.

## Printing Guidelines

:::caution[Important Print Instructions]
1. **Disable "Fit to Page"**: Always print at **100% actual scale**. Scaling down the tag changes the physical marker dimension ($L$), which causes calculated distance measurements from `solvePnP` to be proportionally off.
2. **Preserve the Quiet Zone**: Do not cut inside the black border. The vision detector requires the white quiet zone around the outer black frame to recognize square contours.
:::
